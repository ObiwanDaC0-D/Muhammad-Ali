Version 7 · Research audit

Record integrity

The archive contains 61 professional bouts: 56 wins, including 37 stoppages, and five losses. Bout results have been checked against published fight records. Exact weigh-in weights, venues, and fight times are not asserted where not independently sourced.

V8 corrections: Anthony Esperti name, Jimmy Robinson first-round stoppage, Donnie Fleeman seventh-round stoppage, George Logan fourth-round stoppage, Archie Moore date (November 15, 1962), Jerry Quarry rematch date (June 29, 1972), and Alfredo Evangelista date (May 16, 1977). Historical sources sometimes use KO/TKO/RTD terminology differently; the archive follows the referenced bout records.

Fight dates and outcomes should be checked against historical boxing records; broader historical events against primary and institutional sources. This version includes curated summaries, not invented weigh-in data or unsourced quotations.
